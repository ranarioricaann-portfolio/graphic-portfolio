Case study — Meta Ads Graphics

Explora Journeys — Exceptional Savings Meta Ad

Meta Ads Graphics

1. Project overview

A Meta video ad creative for an Explora Journeys promotion, carrying The Cruise Web logo. The ad promotes an "Exceptional Savings" offer — up to 30% off, plus up to $500 onboard credit — presented as an all-inclusive offer with unlimited beverages, complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi, gratuities, and more, over scenic nature footage. The same creative was produced in four placement sizes so it can run across Meta placements: 1080 × 1030, 1:1, 9:16, and 16:9. Each version runs about 21 seconds.

2. Deliverables

  • Meta video ad, 1080 × 1030 feed — 1080 × 1030 px, MP4, about 22 seconds
  • Meta video ad, 1:1 square — 1080 × 1080 px, MP4, about 22 seconds
  • Meta video ad, 9:16 vertical — 1080 × 1920 px, MP4, about 21 seconds
  • Meta video ad, 16:9 widescreen — 1920 × 1080 px, MP4, about 20 seconds
