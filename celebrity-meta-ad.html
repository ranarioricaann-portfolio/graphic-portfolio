Celebrity Cruises — Limited-Time Offer Meta Ad

Meta Ads Graphics

1. Project overview

A Meta video ad creative for a Celebrity Cruises promotion, carrying The Cruise Web logo. The ad promotes a limited-time offer — up to 75% off the 2nd guest, plus bonus savings on select dates, plus 3rd and 4th guests sail free — over footage of the Celebrity Edge ship. The same creative was produced in four placement sizes so it can run across Meta placements: 4:5, 1:1, 9:16, and 16:9. Each version runs about 16 seconds.

2. Deliverables

  • Meta video ad, 4:5 portrait — 1080 × 1350 px, MP4, about 16 seconds
  • Meta video ad, 1:1 square — 1080 × 1080 px, MP4, about 16 seconds
  • Meta video ad, 9:16 vertical — 1080 × 1920 px, MP4, about 16 seconds
  • Meta video ad, 16:9 widescreen — 1920 × 1080 px, MP4, about 16 seconds
